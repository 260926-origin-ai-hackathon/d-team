# Higashi High School Evacuation Shelter

A designated evacuation shelter of Osaka City (also a tsunami evacuation building). Address: 4-15-14 Higashinoda-cho, Miyakojima-ku, Osaka (north of Kyobashi Station, same block as QUINTBRIDGE).
Note: facilities and locations below are a demo scenario. The shelter office rewrites them in real operation.

## Shelter office (reception)
- Location: the reception table at the gym entrance. Come here whenever you are in trouble or unsure
- If you have not checked in yet, please check in first (you can check in your whole family together)
- Tell the reception if you feel sick, are running out of medicine, or need special care (pregnancy, infants, disability, nursing care, allergies, etc.). We also listen privately
- Staff who speak English may be limited. Show this page or use the chat in English

## Shelter rules
- No shoes inside the gym. Keep your shoes in a bag at your space
- Lights out 22:00, wake up 6:30. Do not leave things in the aisles at night
- No alcohol. No smoking (only at the outdoor smoking area)
- Please keep your valuables with you
- Pets stay at the "pet area" on the north side of the school building, cared for by their owners. Pets cannot enter the gym (guide dogs and service dogs can)
- Tell the reception when you go out or leave the shelter (to keep the list and headcount right)

## Facilities
- Toilets: north side of the gym (men / women) and the 1st floor of the school building. During water outage, follow the posted instructions and use the portable toilet bags
- Women-only space (changing, breastfeeding): small room on the 2nd floor of the gym
- First-aid space: the nurse's office (1st floor of the school building). Visiting hours of public health nurses are posted in the notices
- AED: gym entrance, and in front of the staff room on the 1st floor
- Phone charging: charging corner next to the reception (up to 30 minutes per person)
- Water supply: time and place are posted in the notices
- Wi-Fi: this Wi-Fi is NOT connected to the internet. You can only see information of this shelter

## Nearby evacuation sites (Miyakojima Ward)
- Other shelters in Higashinoda-cho: Sakuranomiya Elementary School (1-10-19 Higashinoda-cho), Sakuranomiya Junior High School (5-16-10 Higashinoda-cho)
- Temporary evacuation sites: Higashinoda Park (1-4), Kyobashi Park (2-6), Sakuranomiya-higashi Park (5-10)
- Wide-area evacuation site (large fires, tsunami, earthquakes): Osaka Castle Park area (south of Kyobashi Station)

## Flood risk in this area (approximate, from the Miyakojima Ward flood hazard map)
- Higashinoda-cho may be deeply flooded by a storm surge (0.5–3 m) or by flooding of the Neya River basin (mainly 1–2 m)
- Tsunami from a Nankai Trough earthquake: Higashinoda-cho is mostly outside the expected area
- Do not leave or move from the shelter until the evacuation order is lifted
- Alert level 4 "Evacuation order": everyone evacuates. Level 5 "Emergency safety": life is in danger

## Where to get help
- Injury, sudden illness, feeling sick: call out loudly to people around you and to the shelter office. Go to the first-aid space. If life-threatening, dial 119 (ambulance)
- Violence, sexual assault, stalking, domestic violence: female staff at the shelter office, or police 110
- Anxiety, cannot sleep: tell the reception. We will connect you with a public health nurse
- Government procedures (disaster victim certificate, support money): the reception will tell you the desk and date (posted in the notices when decided)
- Osaka City Crisis Management Office 06-6208-7384 (if phones work)
