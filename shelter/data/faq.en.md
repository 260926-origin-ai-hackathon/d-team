# Frequently asked questions (for evacuees)

Source: questions found in the shelter-operations research (docs/research/03-information.md §9). The notices and the shelter guide take priority.

## Supplies and food
- Q: Will supplies and food be handed out, or do I go and get them? → A: Time and place of distribution are posted in the notices. If not posted, ask the reception. People staying at home can also get supplies by coming to the reception
- Q: When will the next supplies arrive? → A: Once a delivery is scheduled it is posted in the notices. Sometimes even the shelter office does not know yet
- Q: I saw on social media that supplies arrive at a certain time → A: Social media can be wrong. Check the notices of this shelter
- Q: I have a food allergy → A: Tell the reception. We will check for allergy-friendly food. Also tell the staff at the distribution

## Lifelines and daily life
- Q: When will electricity, water and gas come back? → A: Recovery forecasts are posted in the notices when the shelter office receives them
- Q: When and where is the water supply? → A: See "Water" in the notices. If not posted, ask the reception
- Q: I want to take a bath → A: Bathing support will be posted in the notices when arranged
- Q: I want to charge my phone → A: Use the charging corner next to the reception (up to 30 minutes per person)
- Q: Where is the toilet? How do I use it? → A: North side of the gym and 1st floor of the school building. During water outage, follow the posted instructions and use the portable toilet bags

## Family and safety
- Q: I don't know where my family is → A: Tell the reception the names. We check this shelter's list. Other shelters are checked when we can contact the headquarters
- Q: I want to tell my family I am safe → A: If calls do not go through, the disaster message dial 171 or web171 may work. Also tell the reception

## Procedures
- Q: Where do I apply for the disaster victim certificate? → A: The reception will tell you the desk and date. It is posted in the notices when decided
- Q: Who should I ask? → A: Ask the reception of this shelter first. They will connect you to the right person
- Q: Temporary housing or moving to a hotel? → A: Information sessions are posted in the notices. Ask the reception about conditions (meals, parking, pets)

## Health and medicine
- Q: I have no medicine / not enough → A: Tell the reception or the first-aid space (nurse's office) the name of the medicine and how many doses are left. Bring your medicine notebook if you have one
- Q: I feel sick → A: Go to the first-aid space. If you cannot move, call people around you or the shelter office. If life-threatening, dial 119

## Language and special needs
- Q: Is there an interpreter? → A: There may be no interpreter. This chat works in English, and notices are posted in English too
- Q: I cannot hear the announcements → A: Announcements are also posted as text in the notices on this page. Tell the reception and we will inform you individually

## Disaster information
- Q: What is the latest disaster information? → A: This Wi-Fi is not connected to the internet. Information received by the shelter office is posted in the notices. A radio also helps
- Q: Will a tsunami or flood reach here? → A: See "Flood risk" in the shelter guide. Do not move from the shelter until the evacuation order is lifted
